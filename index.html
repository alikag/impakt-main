Impakt Labs

An operator-led holding company focused on home services.

Impakt Labs is an operator-led holding company focused on owning and improving small, cash-generative home service businesses.

We build durable businesses through disciplined operations, pricing, people systems, and execution — not financial engineering or growth-at-all-costs strategies.

We are not a fund and do not raise blind pools of capital. We invest our own capital first and selectively partner with aligned owners and capital partners over time.

Working With Owners

From time to time, members of our team work with owners of service businesses on short, structured operating reviews.

These engagements are not consulting or implementation work. They are time-boxed diagnostics focused on understanding where execution, pricing, or people systems are limiting performance.

This work is selective and primarily a way to build long-term relationships, compare notes as operators, and stay close to the realities of running service businesses. In some cases, it leads to deeper partnership or ownership discussions.
